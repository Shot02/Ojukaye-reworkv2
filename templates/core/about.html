About Ojukaye

Ojukaye is Nigeria's modern news and discussion platform. We bring together curated reporting from trusted sources, community conversations, and AI-verified credibility signals — all in one place.

What we do

Every day we pull articles from leading Nigerian and international outlets, run them through our verification engine, and publish them in a clean, fast feed. You can filter by category, by source, or by media type. You can like, comment, save, and share — or submit your own news for AI review.

Why it matters

Misinformation spreads fast. We built Ojukaye so readers can spot the difference between a verified report and a rumor at a glance. Every article gets a credibility score. No "Verified" badge means we couldn't confirm it — read it with that in mind.

Who's behind it

Ojukaye is built and maintained by a small team in Nigeria. We're independent, ad-supported, and reader-first. If you spot a bug, want to suggest a feature, or want to partner with us, reach out via the contact page.

Our principles

  • Verification over virality. A post that scores low doesn't get a badge.
  • Reader respect. No auto-play video. No clickbait headlines. No popups.
  • Local first. Nigerian reporting, Nigerian readers, Nigerian context.
  • Transparency. If we get something wrong, we'll say so and fix it.
