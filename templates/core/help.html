Help Center

Answers to the most common questions about using Ojukaye.

Getting started

Do I need an account to read?

No. You can browse all news headlines and previews without signing up. To read full articles, like, comment, or save posts, you'll need a free account.

How do I create an account?

Click Register in the top right, or sign up with Google in one click. We only ask for a username and email.

I forgot my password. What now?

Go to the sign-in page and click "Forgot password?" You'll get an email with a reset link.

Content & verification

What does the "Verified" badge mean?

A "Verified" badge means our AI credibility engine scored the article above 70% based on source reputation, URL safety, content quality, and language. It's a signal, not a guarantee — always read critically.

I found a fake article. How do I report it?

Email news@ojukaye.com with the article link. Our editors review every report.

Can I submit my own news?

Yes. Go to Create Post → News. Paste the source URL and our AI will verify it before publishing.

Using the site

How do I save a post for later?

Click the bookmark icon on any post. Find all your saved posts under your profile menu → Saved Posts.

How do I change the theme?

Open your profile menu (top right) → Theme → pick Default, Original, or Dark. Your choice is saved to your account.

Can I turn off notifications?

Yes — go to Edit Profile → Notifications section.

Still stuck?

Send us a message via the contact page and we'll get back within a few days.
